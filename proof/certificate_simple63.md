## Claim
No bi-colored weighted SIMPLE graph on 6 vertices (bichromatic edges and
complex weights allowed) has all three monochrome IVCs of unit weight with
every other IVC cancelling.  Equivalently (Lean terminology): the equation
system eqSystem6_no_solution_d3 (and d4, d5, ge3) over C has no solution.

## Immediate consequences
- Settles the DeepMind formal-conjectures open statements
  eqSystem6_no_solution_d3 (over C), _d3_real (R), _d3_int (Z),
  _d3_trinary_int ({-1,0,1}) — the argument is over any integral domain.
  NOT settled (and still open in that repo): d4 (4 colors), d5 (5
  colors), ge3 (all D >= 3) and their variants — those require separate
  enumerations over larger color spaces.
- Reproduces and strengthens the mono-edge result of Cervera-Lierta et
  al. (Quantum 6:836) for n=6.
- Leaves open: multigraph (6,3) (parallel edges) and all n >= 8.

## Remaining gap to the conjecture
The Krenn-Gu conjecture allows multigraphs; the simple-case result must be
extended via Q1 (distinct-pair parallel elimination, proof/parallel_edges.md)
or a bounded multigraph search.
