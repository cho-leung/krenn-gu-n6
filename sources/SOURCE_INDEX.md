## Peer-reviewed results on the conjecture

7. Chandran, Gajjala, Illickan — "Krenn-Gu conjecture for sparse graphs" —
   arXiv:2407.00303; MFCS 2024, LIPIcs 306, art. 41, DOI
   10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2024.41. Thm 9 (κ≤2), Thm 10 (κ≤3 reduction → minimal
   counterexample 4-connected), Thm 11/Cor 29 (Δ≤3), Thm 12 (δ=3 ⇒ μ≤3),
   Thm 8 (n=4 via Gröbner), Conjecture 6 (μ≤2).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00303
8. Chandran, Gajjala — "Edge-coloured graphs with only monochromatic perfect
   matchings and their connection to quantum physics" — arXiv:2202.05562;
   Electronic J. Combinatorics 33(3) #P3.21 (2026), DOI 10.37236/12573.
   μ=1/μ=2 characterization + algorithm.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05562
9. Chandran, Gajjala — "Graph-theoretic insights on the constructability of
   complex entangled states" — arXiv:2304.06407; Quantum 8, 1396 (2024),
   DOI 10.22331/q-2024-07-03-1396. Simple graphs: dimension < n/√2; cites
   Ravsky's d ≤ n−2.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06407
10. Cervera-Lierta, Krenn, Aspuru-Guzik — "Design of quantum optical
    experiments with logic artificial intelligence" — Quantum 6, 836 (2022),
    DOI 10.22331/q-2022-10-13-836; arXiv:2109.13273. SAT (MiniSAT, weight-free):
    (6,≥3),(8,≥4) UNSAT in mono-edge setting; K=True ≠ existence caveat.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13273
11. Vardi, Zhang — arXiv:2209.13063 (2022) + "Solving Quantum-Inspired Perfect
    Matching Problems via Tutte's Theorem-Based Hybrid Boolean Constraints",
    IJCAI 2023, pp. 2039–2048 (arXiv:2301.09833). PMVC complexity — related,
    not the conjecture.
